# Awesome Startup Jobs [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of job boards, funding trackers, salary data, and research tools for finding and vetting jobs at venture-backed startups.

Startups hire hardest in the months right after a funding round. This list is organized around that: find who is hiring, find who just raised, check the pay, then vet the company before you sign.

## Startup Job Boards
*General boards focused on startup and tech roles.*

* [Built In](https://builtin.com/jobs) - Tech jobs and company profiles organized by city, with filters for company size and remote work.
* [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/jobs) - Startup job board (formerly AngelList Talent) that shows salary and equity ranges on most listings.
* [Welcome to the Jungle](https://www.welcometothejungle.com) - Curated tech jobs with company culture profiles (absorbed Otta in 2024).
* [Work at a Startup](https://www.workatastartup.com) - Y Combinator's job board for roles at YC-backed companies, with one profile for all applications.

## Jobs at Recently Funded Startups
*Boards that start from the funding round instead of the job post.*

* [JustBacked](https://justbacked.com) - Open roles at startups that raised in the last six months, with the round, lead investors, and the date each role first appeared. Free weekly digest; also searchable from Claude and ChatGPT via MCP.

## Investor Portfolio Job Boards
*Many VC firms run a job board for their portfolio companies.*

* [a16z Portfolio Jobs](https://jobs.a16z.com) - Open roles across Andreessen Horowitz portfolio companies.
* [Sequoia Talent](https://jobs.sequoiacap.com) - Job board for Sequoia Capital portfolio companies.
* [Greylock Portfolio Jobs](https://greylock.com/jobs/portfolio-jobs/) - Open roles at Greylock-backed companies.
* [General Catalyst Jobs](https://jobs.generalcatalyst.com) - Job board for General Catalyst portfolio companies.

## Funding News and Trackers
*Find out who raised, and how much, before the job posts go up.*

* [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com) - Daily funding coverage plus weekly lists of the biggest rounds.
* [SEC EDGAR Form D search](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/#/forms=D) - Public Form D filings, which many US startups file within 15 days of selling shares in a round.
* [TechCrunch Venture](https://techcrunch.com/category/venture/) - Funding announcements and venture industry news.
* [VCBacked](https://vcbacked.co) - Searchable database of venture-backed startups with funding rounds, investors, and hiring signals.

## Salary and Equity Data
*Know the number before the recruiter call.*

* [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi) - Crowdsourced compensation data for tech roles, including startup offers and equity.
* [Startup salaries by funding stage](https://justbacked.com/guides/startup-salaries-by-funding-stage) - Median posted pay by role and funding stage, computed from live US job postings that list a range.
* [Carta: Equity 101](https://carta.com/learn/equity/) - Plain-language guides to stock options, vesting, strike prices, and 83(b) elections.

## Vetting a Startup
*Questions and tools for checking runway, team, and stage before you join.*

* [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com) - Company profiles with funding history, investors, and headcount estimates; free tier covers basic lookups.
* [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com) - Employee reviews and interview reports; thin for very early companies, so weight recent reviews.

## Communities and Threads

* [Hacker News: Who is hiring?](https://news.ycombinator.com/submitted?id=whoishiring) - Monthly thread where startup founders and engineers post open roles directly.
* [r/cscareerquestions](https://www.reddit.com/r/cscareerquestions/) - Large community for software job search, offers, and negotiation.
